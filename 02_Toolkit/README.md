**Project:** Tanasiom Aegis Cyber Essentials Readiness Framework

**Version:** January 2026

**Classification:** SME Cyber Essentials Readiness Toolkit

---

# Overview

The Cyber Essentials Readiness Toolkit is a practical collection of templates, policies, assessment documents, procedures, evidence frameworks, and operational guidance designed to help Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises (SMEs) prepare for Cyber Essentials certification.

The toolkit translates Cyber Essentials requirements into structured, easy-to-follow processes that can be implemented without requiring extensive cybersecurity expertise.

The objective is to bridge the gap between Cyber Essentials guidance and practical implementation by providing organisations with ready-to-use resources that support assessment, remediation, evidence collection, and ongoing compliance activities.

---

# Purpose

This toolkit enables organisations to:

* Assess current Cyber Essentials readiness.
* Identify compliance gaps.
* Prioritise remediation activities.
* Implement key security controls.
* Collect assessment evidence.
* Maintain ongoing compliance.
* Prepare for Cyber Essentials certification.

The toolkit is designed specifically for SME environments and focuses on practicality, usability, and repeatability.

# How an SME Uses This Toolkit

### Step 1 – Complete Initial Assessment

The organisation completes the readiness questionnaire and provides supporting organisational information.

**Output:**

* Baseline readiness profile.
* Initial findings.
* Current-state assessment.

---

### Step 2 – Generate Gap Assessment

Assessment findings are mapped against Cyber Essentials requirements.

**Output:**

* Gap Assessment Report.
* Prioritised findings.
* Recommended remediation actions.

---

### Step 3 – Prioritise Improvements

The organisation focuses on:

* High-risk findings.
* Quick-win improvements.
* Missing Cyber Essentials controls.

**Output:**

* Remediation Plan.
* Action Tracker.
* Ownership assignments.

---

### Step 4 – Collect Evidence

Supporting evidence is gathered using the toolkit's evidence requirements.

Examples include:

* Firewall screenshots.
* MFA configuration evidence.
* Patch reports.
* Device inventories.
* Policy documents.

**Output:**

* Structured Evidence Pack.

---

### Step 5 – Monitoring and Review

Periodic reviews ensure controls remain effective.

Activities include:

* Quarterly reviews.
* User access verification.
* Patch compliance checks.
* Evidence validation.

**Output:**

* Ongoing Cyber Essentials readiness.

# Evidence Framework

The toolkit is designed to generate evidence that may support Cyber Essentials assessments.

* Device inventories.
* Firewall configurations.
* MFA screenshots.
* Patch compliance reports.
* User access reviews.
* Security policies.
* Compliance review records.

Evidence requirements are documented within individual toolkit components and referenced through the Evidence Repository.
