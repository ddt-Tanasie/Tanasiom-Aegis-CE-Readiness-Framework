# Literature Review

## Purpose

The purpose of this section is to critically examine existing academic research, industry standards, government guidance, and cybersecurity best practices relevant to Cyber Essentials compliance, SME cybersecurity challenges, and security readiness frameworks.

The literature review establishes the theoretical and practical foundation upon which the Tanasiom Aegis Cyber Essentials Readiness Framework is developed. It provides evidence-based justification for the project's methodology, design decisions, and overall objectives.

---

## Contents

This section contains:

* Literature review drafts and revisions.
* Academic journal articles.
* Government and industry publications.
* Cyber Essentials guidance documentation.
* SME cybersecurity research.
* Risk assessment and compliance frameworks.
* Harvard-referenced source materials.
* Critical analysis and comparative studies.

## Key Research Areas

### Cyber Essentials Framework

This area examines the structure, objectives, implementation requirements, and effectiveness of the UK Cyber Essentials certification scheme as a baseline cybersecurity standard for organisations.

Research focuses on:

* Cyber Essentials controls and requirements.
* Benefits and limitations of certification.
* Adoption challenges.
* Organisational security improvements following implementation.

### SME Cybersecurity Challenges

This area explores the unique cybersecurity risks and constraints faced by Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises.

Topics include:

* Resource limitations.
* Lack of cybersecurity expertise.
* Financial constraints.
* Low security awareness.
* Barriers to compliance and certification.

### Risk Assessment Methodologies

This section reviews recognised approaches to identifying, assessing, and managing cybersecurity risks.

Areas of investigation include:

* Qualitative and quantitative risk assessment.
* Risk management frameworks.
* Threat modelling techniques.
* Security maturity assessment models.

### Practical Versus Theoretical Security Approaches

Academic frameworks often provide comprehensive security guidance but may be difficult for SMEs to implement in practice.

This review evaluates:

* Real-world applicability of cybersecurity frameworks.
* Usability and accessibility considerations.
* Implementation challenges.
* The balance between security effectiveness and organisational practicality.

### Cybersecurity Readiness Frameworks

Existing readiness and compliance assessment models are analysed to identify strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for improvement.

Particular attention is given to:

* Assessment methodologies.
* Self-evaluation tools.
* Compliance measurement techniques.
* Security benchmarking approaches.

## Role Within the Project

The literature review plays a critical role in ensuring the academic integrity and validity of the project by:

* Establishing a strong theoretical foundation.
* Supporting framework design decisions.
* Identifying current research trends and industry practices.
* Highlighting gaps within existing approaches.
* Justifying the need for an SME-focused Cyber Essentials readiness framework.
* Providing evidence to support recommendations and conclusions.

The findings from this section directly influence the development of the readiness assessment methodology, supporting tools, compliance resources, and overall framework architecture.

---

## Expected Outcomes

The literature review is expected to:

* Identify best practices for Cyber Essentials implementation.
* Highlight common cybersecurity weaknesses within SMEs.
* Evaluate existing readiness and assessment frameworks.
* Reveal limitations in current guidance available to SMEs.
* Provide evidence supporting the development of a simplified and practical compliance solution.
