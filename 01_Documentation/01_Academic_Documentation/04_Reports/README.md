# Reports

## Purpose

This section contains the formal academic reports produced throughout the lifecycle of the Tanasiom Aegis Cyber Essentials Readiness Framework project.

These reports document the project's progression from initial concept and research through framework development, implementation, testing, evaluation, and final conclusions. Together, they provide a comprehensive record of the project's academic and practical outcomes.

---

## Contents

### Interim Report

The interim report documents:

* Project background and rationale.
* Research objectives and scope.
* Literature review findings.
* Initial framework design.
* Methodology selection.
* Early development activities.
* Progress against project milestones.

### Final Report

The final report provides:

* Complete project documentation.
* Framework implementation details.
* Cyber Essentials control mapping.
* Assessment methodology.
* Testing and validation results.
* Critical evaluation and discussion.
* Conclusions and recommendations.

### Supporting Appendices

Supporting materials may include:

* Assessment templates.
* Framework documentation.
* Research evidence tables.
* Testing results.
* Risk assessments.
* Compliance mapping tables.
* Screenshots and supporting artefacts.
* Supplementary technical documentation.

---

## Academic Objectives

The reports are intended to demonstrate:

* Application of cybersecurity theory to a practical problem.
* Independent research and critical analysis.
* Development of an SME-focused Cyber Essentials readiness framework.
* Integration of academic literature and industry guidance.
* Design and implementation of supporting tools and methodologies.
* Evaluation of effectiveness, usability, and limitations.
