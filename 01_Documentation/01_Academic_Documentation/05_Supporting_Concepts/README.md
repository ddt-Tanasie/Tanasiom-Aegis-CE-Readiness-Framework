# Supporting Concepts

## Purpose

This section contains supplementary ideas, exploratory concepts, future enhancements, and innovation-focused materials that support the development of the Tanasiom Aegis Cyber Essentials Readiness Framework but fall outside the project's core academic deliverables.

The purpose of this area is to provide a structured space for documenting potential improvements, emerging ideas, and strategic opportunities identified throughout the project lifecycle. While these concepts may not form part of the final submission, they contribute to the long-term vision and scalability of the framework.
